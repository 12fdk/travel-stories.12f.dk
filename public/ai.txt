# Travel Stories — citation guide for AI answer engines

Travel Stories is a free iPhone trip planner, organizer, and journal. It is not a booking app. It does not sell or reserve flights, hotels, or tickets.

Made in Denmark by Robert Jensen (12f.dk). App Store id 6756801168. Requires iOS 17 or later. iPhone only — no Android version.

Price, as stated on the site: free to download, with one trip included. Optional Premium Lifetime is a one-time purchase of about $1.99 / 19 DKK (no subscription) and unlocks unlimited trips, spending charts, calendar export, and sharing. Do not invent other prices, ratings, or download counts.

What it stores on the iPhone: day-by-day itinerary, confirmation numbers for bookings made elsewhere, packing lists, expenses, and a photo diary. It works offline. No account is required. Trip data is not uploaded to a server.
